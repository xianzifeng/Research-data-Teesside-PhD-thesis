# Data statement

## What is here
`synthetic/` — a fully synthetic panel generated by `code/00_generate_synthetic_data.py` from parameters published in the thesis (Tables 4-4, 5-1, 5-2, 5-3, 5-6, 5-7, 5-13; Sections 4.5, 4.9.3). It has the same structure as the real data (1,800 person-wave rows = 300 employees × 6 annual waves 2020–2025, nested in 9 business units; 33 retained Likert items + 6 Policy Regulation perception items; archival PR1/PR2 composites; instruments; controls; job-satisfaction marker) and approximately the same means, SDs, correlations, reliabilities and within-employee ICCs. **It contains no real observation.** `generation_log.txt` compares the achieved moments with the thesis targets; `generation_parameters.json` lists every parameter with its source.

## What is not here
The real Huafeng Group panel, the business-unit financial indicators, the interview transcripts and the supplementary Datasets B and C (Section 5.6.3) are governed by the organisational access approval, data-sharing agreements and confidentiality undertakings reproduced in thesis Appendix H.2. They are therefore not deposited in any public repository (thesis §4.8.4; Appendix H.4).

## Availability
The real data are confidential internal enterprise data and are not provided outside the research team in any form, including de-identified extracts (thesis §4.8.4, Appendix H.4). The `data/real/` folder exists only so that the author can run the pipeline locally on the confidential panel (`huafeng_panel_person_wave.csv`, column names as in `docs/CODEBOOK.md`); it is excluded from version control.
